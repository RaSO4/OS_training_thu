## Notes
- vector batch=1 is the best single-process config.  batch=8 is slower per sample
  (6906 vs 2154 ticks) because the batched dot-product path does not pipeline the
  per-output reductions as well; batch is not used for the final benchmark.
- in-process threads crash on StarryOS (TLS/thread startup bug, VA 0x10/0x0),
  independent of RVV and affinity; multi-process pinning is the stable scaling path.
- sched_getcpu returns -14; affinity itself is effective (A100/X100 timing split and
  linear multi-process scaling).

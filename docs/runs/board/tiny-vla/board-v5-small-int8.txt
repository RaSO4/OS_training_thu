## Final configuration
- X100: vector small int8, one process per core, `--quant i8 --batch 1 --threads 1`.
- A100: scalar small int8 is stable but slow; plain RVV is unstable under
  preemption.  A100 belongs on the IME path, not this kernel.
- In-process threads still fail at startup (StarryOS thread/TLS); multi-process
  pinning is the stable scaling mechanism.
